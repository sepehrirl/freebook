## Service
FreeBook provides an online catalogue and reader for books that are in the public domain or whose rights holders have authorized free distribution.

## Copyright and licensing
A book being free to read does not automatically mean it may be copied or redistributed. Every published item must have a verified public-domain basis, an open license that permits the intended use, or documented permission from the rights holder. Source and attribution details must be shown with the book.

Users must keep account credentials private and provide accurate account information. The operator may restrict accounts involved in abuse, unlawful activity, or attempts to bypass access controls, subject to applicable law.
