# Privacy notice draft

**Status: draft for review — not a legal notice.** The operator must complete the contact details, hosting region, retention rules, age policy, and jurisdiction-specific review before public launch.

## Information the service currently needs
- Account email, handled by Supabase Authentication.
- Display name, stored in the FreeBook profile.
- Bookmarks and reading-progress records, stored to provide a personal library.
- Basic technical and authentication logs generated by the hosting and backend providers.

FreeBook does not currently require a public profile, payment information, precise location, or advertising identifiers.

## Purpose
The information above is used to create and secure accounts, provide sign-in, remember saved books, and synchronize reading progress.

## Service providers
The static site is intended to be hosted by GitHub Pages. Account and database functions are provided by the Supabase project configured by the operator. The operator must publish the actual hosting region and applicable provider privacy links before launch.

## User choices
A production release must provide a clear process to request account access, correction, and deletion. Self-service account deletion is not implemented yet and is a launch blocker. Do not collect more personal information than the service needs.

## Children and age requirements
Before launch, the operator must define a minimum-age and parental-consent policy appropriate to the countries where the service will be offered. Do not launch account registration to children in a market where required consent or safeguards are not in place.

## Contact
Add a monitored privacy contact address before launch.

## Changes
Update this notice when data practices or service providers change. Obtain legal review for the target markets before treating this draft as final.
